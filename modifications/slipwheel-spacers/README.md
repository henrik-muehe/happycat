# Slipwheel mounting spacers

Two simple cylindrical spacers for the newer **Grabner Happy Cat Star slipwheel mounting brackets**.

## The problem

The metal mounting bracket can stay permanently on the rear crossmember, but without the slipwheel arm installed, only one bolt holds it in place and the other can slip out. Simply fitting the original nut can leave things sitting awkwardly when the hulls are inflated.

The goal is to keep both bolts securely in place, ready to fit the slipwheels even if you forgot about them before inflating the boat.

## The fix

Two cylinders, each the same thickness as the slipwheel arm, with a through-hole for the bolt. They take the arm's place when it is not installed, so the bolts can be tightened properly.

Leave the bracket and both bolts on the rear crossmember, with the spacers fitted. When you need the slipwheels, remove the spacers and fit the arms in their place. No loose bolt to lose or mounting hardware to remember before inflation.
